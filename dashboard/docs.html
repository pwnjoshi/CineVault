1. Core System Architecture

CineVault Studio eliminates the multi-week archival clearance bottleneck for film editors, documentary directors, and newsrooms. The platform indexes 15 Connected Institutional Repositories with multi-agent intelligence and NLE real-time event streaming:

Connected Archival Libraries

  • National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) — US Federal agency records cleared under 17 U.S.C. § 105.
  • Library of Congress (LOC) — National Film Registry & historic motion picture telecine scans.
  • NASA Media & Spaceflight Archive — Official Apollo mission footage, launch telemetry & spaceflight records.
  • Prelinger Archives & FedFlix Collection — Over 60,000 educational, industrial & atomic energy films.
  • British Film Institute (BFI National Archive) — 20th-century British industrial history, WWII newsreels & silent film masters.

  • Imperial War Museums Film Archive (IWM) — WWI/WWII 35mm newsreels & historic military telecine masters.
  • National Film Board of Canada (NFB / ONF) — 80+ years of award-winning documentary & historical film preservation masters.
  • Australian National Film and Sound Archive (NFSA) — Australasian historical newsreels & 20th-century cultural heritage preservation masters.
  • Swedish Film Institute Archival Vault (Filmarkivet) — Early silent film era & European mid-century documentary telecine scans.
  • US National Library of Medicine Historical Film Vault (NLM) — 19th & 20th century medical, scientific, and public health film archives.
  • Danish Film Institute Nitrate Restoration Vault (DFI) — Early 1900s nitrate film restoration catalog.

3. Autonomous Script-to-Timeline AI

Located under the Script to Timeline workspace tab:

  1. Paste any screenplay excerpt (e.g. SCENE 1: INT. CAPE CANAVERAL LAUNCH CONTROL - 1969 - DAWN...) or click Preset: Apollo 11 Mission.
  2. Click Deconstruct & Source Timeline. The AI deconstructs scene headings, calculates cut durations (Mark In / Mark Out), and fetches archival candidate clips for every shot.
  3. Preview candidate clips inline or click Export Premiere XML Timeline to generate a multi-track sequence ready for NLE import!

4. AI Voiceover-to-Timeline B-Roll Auto-Cutter

Endpoint: POST /api/audio-to-timeline

Parses narration voiceover audio transcripts and MP3 timestamp files. The AI extracts sentence-level visual keywords, matches period-accurate B-roll clips, and builds a 2-track timeline with voiceover audio on Track A1 and matched archival B-roll on Track V1.

5. Gemini Multimodal Visual Moodboard Matcher

Endpoint: POST /api/image-search

Click Moodboard Matcher in the search bar or select a sample frame preset (e.g. Apollo 11 Launch, 1962 Assembly Line, 80s Shibuya Tech). Gemini Pro Vision analyzes image color palettes, lighting, grain structure, and historical artifacts to retrieve visually matching footage.

6. Cinema Viewfinder & Film Stock Emulation Shaders

Clicking ▶ Open Cinema Viewfinder on any clip card launches the interactive theater stage:

  • Aspect Ratio Masks: Toggle between 16:9 Flat, 4:3 Academy, and 2.39:1 Scope anamorphic framing.
  • Real-Time LUT Shaders: Apply authentic film stock color grading profiles on the fly (Kodachrome, 16mm Silver B&W, Technicolor, Bleach Bypass).
  • Import Graded Clip to Premiere Pro: Click the orange button to package your active LUT grade, aspect mask, and timecodes into clip metadata and stream directly to Premiere Pro!

9. Adobe Premiere Pro UXP Real-Time Sync Pipeline

CineVault offers two integration modes for Adobe Premiere Pro CC 2022–2025:

Mode A: Native Adobe UXP Extension Panel

  1. Open Adobe UXP Developer Tool.
  2. Click Add Plugin and select premiere-panel/manifest.json.
  3. Click Load. Open in Premiere Pro under Window > Extensions > CineVault Footage Finder.
  4. Clips shortlisted in the web studio or panel automatically sync in real time via Server-Sent Events (SSE /api/live-sync).

Mode B: Multi-Format NLE Sequence Exporters

Export your shortlist to any professional NLE system:

  • Adobe Premiere Pro XML (.xml): Preserves tracks, timecodes, and LUT notes.
  • CMX 3600 Edit Decision List (.edl): Standard EDL for online finishing.
  • DaVinci Resolve / Final Cut Pro (.fcpxml): FCPXML sequence interchange.
  • Production CSV (.csv): Metadata spreadsheet for cue sheets and rights tracking.
